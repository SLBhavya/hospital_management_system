About Dr. Anil Rajeshwar:

Dr. Rajeshwar is an acclaimed cardiothoracic surgeon whose passion for innovation in healthcare has transformed the landscape of heart surgery in India. After obtaining his medical degree from AIIMS, New Delhi, he pursued specialized training in cardiothoracic surgery at the prestigious Johns Hopkins University in the USA. His vision to make advanced cardiac care affordable and accessible led to the founding of Sunshine Medical Institute.

Under his leadership, the hospital has grown into a state-of-the-art facility providing world-class healthcare. Known for his patient-centric approach, Dr. Rajeshwar advocates for the integration of technology and medicine, pioneering telemedicine services and digital consultations, particularly benefiting patients in rural regions.

With a deep commitment to healthcare reform and innovation, Dr. Rajeshwar continues to inspire the next generation of doctors through mentorship, research, and global collaborations.
