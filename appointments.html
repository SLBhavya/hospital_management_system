Booking an Appointment at Hospital Mangement System Website

1. How to Book an Appointment

  • Step 1: Log In
    • Visit the Apollo Hospital Hyderabad website.
    • Click on the "Login" button located at the top right corner of the page.
    • Enter your registered email address and password to access your account. If you are a new user, you can register by clicking on the "Sign Up" link.
  • Step 2: Access the Appointment Page
    • After logging in, navigate to the "Appointments" section from the main dashboard or menu.
    • You will be directed to the appointment booking page.
  • Step 3: Choose Your Appointment Type
    • Routine Appointments: Select the department and the doctor you wish to consult. You can view available slots and choose a convenient time.
    • Emergency Appointments: For urgent consultations, choose the "Emergency Appointment" option. Emergency slots are available for immediate attention and are prioritized based on urgency.
  • Step 4: Provide Patient Information
    • Enter your personal details, including your contact number, medical history, and any specific concerns or symptoms.
  • Step 5: Confirm and Book
    • Review your appointment details.
    • Click "Book Appointment" to confirm your booking.
    • You will receive a confirmation message and an email with the appointment details.

2. Emergency Appointments

  • Booking: Emergency appointments can be booked directly through the "Emergency Appointment" option on the appointment page.
  • Prioritization: Emergency appointments are given priority to ensure immediate medical attention. Availability may vary based on the severity of the case and hospital resources.

3. Waiting Time

  • Routine Appointments: The waiting time for routine appointments may vary based on the doctor’s schedule and the current patient load. It is advisable to book in advance to secure your preferred time slot.
  • Emergency Appointments: The waiting time for emergency appointments is minimized, but it may still vary depending on the urgency of cases and hospital capacity.
